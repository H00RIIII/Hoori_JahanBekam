## ✦ About

I am a **visual communication designer and creative director** focused on building cohesive visual identities across print, digital, and event environments.

As the founder of **Studio Kam**, I work on brand systems, editorial and packaging design, event visuals, WordPress-based digital experiences, and social media content. My approach brings strategy, typography, and visual storytelling together to create consistent brand experiences.
